Auto Fill Documents - Privacy Policy

Introduction

This privacy policy explains how Auto Fill Documents handles your data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your information.

Data Collection and Usage

Auto Fill Documents operates entirely on your local machine and does not:

  • Collect personal information
  • Track usage data
  • Upload documents or data to external servers
  • Share any information with third parties

Document Access

The application requires access to:

  • Word documents (.doc, .docx) that you explicitly select
  • Excel files (.xls, .xlsx, .xlsm) that you explicitly select
  • Local storage for saving output documents

All document processing occurs locally on your device.

Data Storage

The application:

  • Creates temporary files during document processing
  • Automatically removes temporary files after processing
  • Stores output documents only in locations you specify
  • Does not maintain any permanent data storage

Security

We implement security measures including:

  • Local-only processing of all documents
  • Proper cleanup of temporary files
  • Secure handling of COM objects
  • No network connectivity required or used

Updates

This privacy policy may be updated to reflect changes in the application's functionality. Users will be notified of any significant changes through application updates.
